## The two judgement calls

1. **"a red-flecked salt rim"** on five of the six drinks frames. The rims are visibly red and granular. Naming the ingredient (chili salt, Tajin) would be an inference from a photograph, so the alt describes the appearance instead. If the bar confirms what it is, the alt can say so.

2. **"a white ceramic cup of pale green tea"** on the three house mixed drinks frames. It is a green liquid in a white ceramic cup. Calling it green tea is a reasonable read of a Japanese restaurant's table and it is the only non-alcoholic item in those frames, which is worth a reader knowing. If anyone objects, "a white ceramic cup of a pale green drink" is the neutral version.
